# `func_80048900` reconstruction notes

This routine builds a movement request for a vehicle. It follows the current
waypoint until arrival, then optionally acquires a target, checks its range and
line of sight, turns toward it, and resumes full speed.

The candidate in `80048900_candidate.c` recovers the complete control flow,
structures, fixed-point multiply, calls, and global accesses. It emits 512
bytes for the 508-byte retail extent, with only zero padding after the extent.
It differs in 69 of 127 instruction words, improving the earlier 74-word
candidate.

Confirmed stack layout:

- movement delta at `sp + 24`
- range probe at `sp + 40`
- 40-byte contact result at `sp + 56`
- 120-byte total frame

The remaining differences are concentrated in two compiler-shape issues:

1. Retail keeps the vehicle in `s3` and the acquired target in `s2`. GCC 2.8.1
   assigns those two values in the opposite order. The probe pointer, position
   pointer, and movement request already occupy the correct `s0`, `s1`, and
   `s4` registers.
2. Retail expands the `0x200` status update into a shared-speed block containing
   a jump and a duplicated `4096` load. The candidate merges that branch and is
   two instructions shorter.

The empty register constraint on `probe_ptr` is deliberate. It gives the probe
address the lifetime needed for `s0` while retaining retail's direct
stack-relative initialization stores. Routing those stores through a helper
instead raises the mismatch count to 74 words.

This file is research-only and is not included in the exact-unit manifest.
